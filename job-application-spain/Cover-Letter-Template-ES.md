## 💡 Tips for Customizing Your Cover Letter

### Research the Company
1. Visit the company website and read their "About Us" section
2. Check their latest blog posts or news
3. Look at their tech stack on job postings
4. Find their GitHub repositories if they're open source
5. Read employee reviews on Glassdoor

### Personalize Each Letter
1. **Use the hiring manager's name** if possible (check LinkedIn)
2. **Reference specific projects or products** the company has developed
3. **Match keywords** from the job description
4. **Highlight relevant experience** that matches their needs
5. **Show genuine interest** in their mission and values

### Structure Best Practices
- **Keep it to one page** (max 400-500 words)
- **Use 3-4 paragraphs maximum**
- **Be specific** with examples and achievements
- **Show enthusiasm** but remain professional
- **Include a call to action** in the conclusion

### Common Mistakes to Avoid
❌ Generic letters sent to multiple companies  
❌ Repeating everything from your CV  
❌ Focusing only on what you want, not what you offer  
❌ Typos or grammatical errors  
❌ Being too formal or too casual  
❌ Making it too long  

### Do's
✅ Research the company thoroughly  
✅ Customize for each application  
✅ Show how you can add value  
✅ Be specific with examples  
✅ Proofread multiple times  
✅ Keep it concise and engaging
